Mathematics education

Teaching

I am a mathematics teacher in secondary education. My teaching interests include helping students build conceptual understanding, develop mathematical reasoning, and approach unfamiliar problems with confidence.

01 / ROLE

Mathematics teacher

Teaching mathematics at the secondary-school level. Institutional appointments, teaching dates, and specific courses can be added to this profile when finalized.

02 / APPROACH

Problem-solving and active learning

Emphasis on student participation, discovery-oriented activities, mathematical discussion, and carefully scaffolded problem solving.

03 / EXPLANATION

Conceptual understanding

Connecting symbolic procedures with visual representations, examples, and the reasoning behind mathematical methods.

04 / MATERIALS

Structured learning resources

Preparing detailed worked solutions, mathematical notes, and typeset materials to support classroom learning and independent study.

Teaching philosophy

Make mathematical thinking visible

I aim to present mathematics as a connected way of thinking: students should understand not only how a method works, but also why it is appropriate and when it can be applied.
